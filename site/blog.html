The Colour of Magic

Rincewind ran where sensible men stayed,

While Twoflower smiled and blithely paid.

A tourist wandered, purse in hand,

Through dragons, trolls, and shifting sand.

The world itself seemed bent on flight,

With Death and fire and stolen light.

And everywhere the wizard knew:

The safest plan was simply flee.

The Light Fantastic

Two worlds collide beneath the sky,

While Rincewind has no wish to die.

The stars grow strange, the magic bends,

And Discworld's fate on chance depends.

Cohen grows old but will not yield,

While wizards clash across the field.

A sourceless spell, a world at stake—

And Rincewind's running for his sake.

Equal Rites

A wizard's staff to Granny came,

And with it magic, rank, and name.

But magic chose young Esk instead,

Though no one wanted girls ahead.

With Granny Weatherwax at her side,

She crossed where wizard rules divide.

At last the ancient powers knew:

The gift of magic belonged to who?

Mort

Mort took a job with Death one day,

And learned that souls don't wait or stay.

But when he tried to change one fate,

The universe would not cooperate.

A princess lived whom Death had marked,

While Mort's own heart was fiercely sparked.

With Death, a sword, and time undone,

He learned that life can't simply be won.

Sourcery

The eighth son came with magic grand,

And spells that shook the very land.

Young Coin arrived with power bright,

And nearly drowned the world in night.

Rincewind fled as wizards fought,

While heroes battled what they'd wrought.

For magic's greatest lesson, strange but true:

Too much of anything will ruin you.

Wyrd Sisters

Three witches watched a kingdom fall,

As murder echoed through the hall.

A crown was stolen, a child concealed,

While old, crooked justice slowly healed.

Granny schemed and Nanny laughed,

Magrat found the courage in her path.

Through prophecy, theatre, fate, and weather,

The rightful king came home at last together.

Pyramids

A prince came home with doubts to face,

To rule a kingdom stuck in place.

The pyramids grew tall and strange,

While ancient customs resisted change.

Assassins waited, prophecies spun,

And Death found work beneath the sun.

Then history bent beneath one dream:

Sometimes the past is not supreme.

Guards! Guards!

A dragon came to rule the town,

While secret hands sought Ankh-Morpork's crown.

The Watch was small, corrupt, and slow,

Until a stubborn man said, "No."

Carrot marched with honest might,

Vimes discovered how to fight.

And when the dragon's reign was through,

The Watch found something else to do.

Excercise 3
Key Terms From Chapter 2

External style sheet

An external style sheet saved as a separate text document but linked to from the webpage it modifies.

ID selector

A specific type of Cascading Style Sheets selector intended to be used once on a webpage. It begins with the # symbol.

Inline style

A formatting declaration created in the HTML tag being formatted.

List item

One item inside an ordered or unordered HTML list.

Nested

HTML tags that are created between other HTML tags.

Ordered list

HTML tag set used to create a numbered or lettered list of similar items, in order.

Relative links

Links that do not contain the full file path to a webpage.

Unordered lists

HTML tag set used to create a bulleted list of items.

Version control

Software that is used to create date-time stamped copies of coding projects.

WYSIWYG

An editor that shows the user "What You See Is What You Get."
