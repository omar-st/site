Anarchism is a political philosophy and movement that seeks to abolish all institutions that perpetuate authority, coercion, or hierarchy; primarily targeting the state, capitalism, and organized religion. Anarchism advocates for the replacement of hierarchical social structures with stateless societies consisting of voluntary associations
